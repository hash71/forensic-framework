## Mechanical consistency check

Before every release, extract `\\cite{...}` keys from `paper.tex`, split
multi-citations, and compare the sorted set with BibTeX keys. The build must
also complete without an undefined-citation warning. Unused bibliography
entries are permitted in the source file but do not count as audited citations
until the manuscript cites them.
